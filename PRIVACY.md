# Privacy policy

SquirrelWorks publishes the Android apps named below. This policy covers all of them. Contact: brianhack@hackingcovfefe.com. Source and website: https://github.com/squirelworks/LanguageStudentDesk.

## Apps

- Programming Student Desk (`com.squirelworks.programmingstudentdesk`)
- Language Student Desk (`com.squirelworks.lsd`)
- SquirrelGo Alphabets (`com.squirelworks.squirrelgoalphabets`)
- SquirrelGo Kana (`com.squirelworks.squirrelgokana`)
- SquirrelGo Kanji (`com.squirelworks.squirrelgokanji`)
- SquirrelGo Klingon (`com.squirelworks.squirrelgoklingon`)
- SquirrelGo Orthodox Bible (`com.squirelworks.squirrelgoorthodoxbible`)
- SquirrelGo Phrasebook (`com.squirelworks.squirrelgophrasebook`)
- SquirrelGo Japanese-English Dictionary (`com.squirelworks.squirrelgodictionary`)

## What the apps do

These are offline study apps. Lessons, texts, dictionary entries, and phrase packs are stored in the app and shown in a WebView. There is no SquirrelWorks account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics SDK, and no cloud sync of your progress.

## Data SquirrelWorks collects

SquirrelWorks does not collect personal information from these apps. The apps do not send analytics, crash reports, advertising IDs, location, contacts, or account data to SquirrelWorks. There is no SquirrelWorks server that the apps report to.

## Data stored on the device

Study progress, quiz scores, favorites, bookmarks, highlights, notes, tags, history, and similar state are stored on the device (WebView local storage). Extra Bible or commentary packs that you choose to download in SquirrelGo Orthodox Bible are stored on the device (IndexedDB). That data stays on the device. SquirrelWorks does not receive it.

If Android backup is turned on for the device, the system may include an app's local data in the backup of your own Google account. That backup is performed by Android. It is not sent to SquirrelWorks.

## Network

Programming Student Desk, Language Student Desk, SquirrelGo Alphabets, SquirrelGo Kana, SquirrelGo Kanji, SquirrelGo Klingon, SquirrelGo Phrasebook, and SquirrelGo Japanese-English Dictionary do not declare the `INTERNET` permission. They do not download content themselves.

Language Student Desk and Programming Student Desk can hand an `https` link to your browser (for example a resource page). SquirrelGo Alphabets, Kana, Kanji, Klingon, Phrasebook, Dictionary, and Orthodox Bible can do the same from the suite sheet (for example a reference page or a related download). The browser loads that page. The app does not receive the page contents.

SquirrelGo Orthodox Bible declares `INTERNET` for one purpose: if you choose to download an extra Bible or commentary pack, the app fetches that public text file over HTTPS from GitHub (`raw.githubusercontent.com`) and saves it on the device. The request is a file download. Notes, bookmarks, and highlights are not uploaded.

## Audio, speech, microphone, and location

Some apps ship pronunciation clips inside the app package (kana, alphabets, Klingon letters and example words, and phrasebook lines). Playing those clips does not use the microphone and does not record you.

Where a lesson or verse is spoken, the app uses the device text-to-speech engine if a voice for that language is installed. Speech audio is not recorded or uploaded.

None of these apps request the microphone, camera, location, or contacts.

## Children

These apps are study tools for a general audience, ages 13 and older. They are not directed at children under 13 and are not offered as Designed for Families apps.
