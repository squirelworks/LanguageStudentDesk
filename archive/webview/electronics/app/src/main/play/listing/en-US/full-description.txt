Electronics Student Desk is the Electronics Learner from Language Student Desk as its own offline Android app.

Pick a course from the home menu — the same subject sections and A–Z view as on the Desk — and work through lessons on your phone. Everything ships in the app. There is no account, no ads, and no cloud sync.

Courses
• Foundations
• Components
• Circuits
• Digital
• Soldering
• PCB design
• Systems

What you get
• Lessons with worked examples
• Practice questions kept on the device
• The same course packs the Desk uses
• Fully offline once installed

Privacy
No account, no ads, no tracking. The app collects no data. See the privacy policy linked from this listing.
