# SquirrelGo v0.1.7

Linux, Windows and macOS installers are on this release for every native
app and desk. Each one has a `.deb`, a `.dmg` and an `.msi`. They are
unsigned, so your system may ask you to confirm before opening one.
SquirrelGo 国語辞典 and SquirrelGo Multilingual Dictionary also have their
APKs on this release.

Each app on Google Play has a newer version, so it installs over the
v0.1.6 build. The lessons are the same as v0.1.6.

## Downloads

The APKs for every app are attached here, along with `SHA256SUMS.txt`. The
Linux, Windows and macOS builds are attached as each one finishes. Each app
except SquirrelGo 国語辞典 and SquirrelGo Multilingual Dictionary also goes to
its Google Play closed testing track; those two ship as APKs here.
