## Known gaps

Be honest when publishing; drop or rewrite lines once the matching PRs have landed.

- **Handoff not on `main` yet.** Until `#117`–`#146` merge, a `v0.0.5` cut from current `main` would be identical to v0.0.4 plus this notes file. Prefer waiting for at least P0 + P1 (and ideally P2 resource/translation + P3 depth/Driving) before tagging.
- **Cherokee / Inuktitut / Manipuri** still lack grammar packs on `main`; their vocab and Cherokee’s reference pack are still the thin v0.0.4 numbers until `#119`–`#126` land.
- **Resource Learner** still has dedicated panels only for the v0.0.4 set (general, japanese, klingon, arabic, hangul, chinese, hindi, cyrillic, latin, english) until `#128`–`#131` merge.
- **Translation Learner** still has seven columns on `main` until `#132`–`#139` merge.
- **Hanzi/hanja stroke order:** Character Learner reuses KanjiVG by codepoint where glyphs match; many Simplified and education-hanja forms still show no stroke data, and CJK does not mount the checked writing pad. `#140` records the investigation; importing Make Me a Hanzi (or similar) is still future work.
- **Signed builds:** APKs remain debug; desktop builds remain unsigned (Windows SmartScreen / macOS Gatekeeper warnings). Release-signed APKs and signed Desk builds need the owner’s keystore / certificates as repo secrets — do not generate or commit keys from an agent.
- **CI coverage thresholds** (once `#118` lands) are informational only; failing a `--min` floor does not block merges yet.
- **English** remains the base language (reference only, no grammar/vocab pack) by design.
- **README sweep** (P4) after the batch merges so root and app READMEs match the new counts.
