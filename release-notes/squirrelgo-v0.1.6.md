# SquirrelGo v0.1.6

Every SquirrelGo app and desk is a native Kotlin and Compose app, and each
one has its own version. The old WebView shells are archived and are not
built.

That is SquirrelGo Kana, SquirrelGo Bopomofo, SquirrelGo Alphabets, SquirrelGo
Kanji, SquirrelGo Klingon, SquirrelGo Japanese-English Dictionary, SquirrelGo
国語辞典, SquirrelGo Multilingual Dictionary, SquirrelGo Phrasebook, SquirrelGo
Orthodox Bible, Language Student Desk, Programming Student Desk, Maths
Edu-Training Helper and Electronics Student Desk. The lessons stay in each
desk and in Language Student Desk.

## Desktop

Linux, Windows and macOS packages ship for every native app and desk,
including Language Student Desk, Programming Student Desk, Maths
Edu-Training Helper and Electronics Student Desk. The builds are unsigned,
so your system may ask you to confirm before opening one.

## Downloads

The APKs for every app are attached here, along with `SHA256SUMS.txt`. The
Linux, Windows and macOS builds are attached as each one finishes. Each app
except SquirrelGo 国語辞典 and SquirrelGo Multilingual Dictionary also goes to
its Google Play closed testing track; those ship as APKs here.
